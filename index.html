Quick key guide

RPN: enter the first number, press ENTER, enter the next number, then choose +, −, × or ÷. For example: 12 ENTER 3 ÷.

Gold functions: press f, then the key. Blue functions: press g, then the key.

TVM: enter values and press n, i, PV, PMT or FV to store them. After storing the known values, press the key for the value to solve.

Memory: STO then a digit stores X; RCL then a digit recalls it. The calculator keeps its stack, financial registers and memory on this device.

Haptics: every on-screen key sends a short vibration when supported. The phone's system vibration setting controls its strength.

Note: this is an independent 12C-style companion, not an official HP product. Some advanced original functions (such as keystroke programming and bond/depreciation routines) are not implemented.
